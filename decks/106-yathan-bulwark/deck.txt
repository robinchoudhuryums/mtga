#: archetype: 100-card Brawl, ABZAN TOUGHNESS around Hamza, Might of the Yathan. Every land you play SEEKS a creature
#: archetype: card and manifests it face down as a 2/2; when any face-down creature is turned face up, Hamza makes it
#: archetype: ENDURE X where X is its toughness (X +1/+1 counters, or an X/X Spirit). So the deck is built from creatures
#: archetype: with LOW cost and HIGH toughness (Crystal Barricade flips for 2 and endures 4, Flatman 4 → 10, The Walls of
#: archetype: Ba Sing Se 8 → 30, flipped for {1}{G} by Hauntwoods Shrieker), and toughness becomes the damage stat
#: archetype: through the enablers (Felothar the Steadfast, Assault Formation, Huatli, Doran, Bedrock
#: archetype: Tortoise, Ghalta, Teyo). Two loops: Ashaya makes every face-down manifest a land (landfall
#: archetype: → another manifest, until the library runs out of creatures) and Threats Around Every Corner fetches a
#: archetype: basic for each face-down creature (landfall → another manifest). Elesh Norn doubles every landfall. Low
#: archetype: power is UPSIDE: power-capped recursion (Smile at Death, Slumbering Walker, Nethroi's total power 10)
#: archetype: returns the walls, Delney makes power ≤2 attackers unblockable by power ≥3, and Destined Confrontation,
#: archetype: Choking Miasma and Toxic Deluge are one-sided against a board of 5+ toughness. Distinct from every roster deck:
#: archetype: the only face-down / toughness-as-damage list.
#: protect: Hamza, Might of the Yathan; Felothar the Steadfast; Doran, Besieged by Time; Assault Formation; Crystal Barricade; The Walls of Ba Sing Se; Hauntwoods Shrieker; Threats Around Every Corner; Ashaya, Soul of the Wild; Smile at Death; Delney, Streetwise Lookout; Destined Confrontation
#: notes: Drafted 2026-11-24 from the Abzan toughness pile (.cycle/abzan-toughness-pile-analysis.md) plus an off-pile
#: notes: search, then tuned on its Builder board (builds/hamza) the same day: both loops at moderate depth (38
#: notes: creatures, 12 basics). A flipped creature gets no ETB, no kicker and no cost reduction, but a manifest never
#: notes: HAS to be flipped, so Towering Titan (0/0 if flipped) stays a 2/2 face down and is cast for its counters.
#: notes: Face-down 2/2s die to our own Choking Miasma / Toxic Deluge: flip the ones worth keeping first (each flip is
#: notes: an endure). The Notary Hobbits makes about 9 colourless a turn (25 with Elesh Norn) for the big flips.
