#~ -Emeritus of Woe | +Pinnacle Starcage | CRAFT (R, EOE — not near rotation): {1}{W}{W} artifact whose ETB exiles ALL artifacts and creatures with mana value 2 or less. The wipe is symmetric, so the question is how many of YOUR permanents it catches: measured 2026-09-01 across all 36 white decks, this deck has 5 (Night Nurse, Stone Docent, Moonshadow, Undercover Skrull, Judge Magister Gabranth) and the FEWEST token makers on the roster at 1 — tokens are mana value 0, so a token deck loses its whole board permanently and this deck loses almost nothing. White is 10 sources here, so {W}{W} clears the pip-depth check. Adds interaction 7 -> 8 (it classifies as Sweeper).
#~ note: THE SECOND ABILITY IS WHY THIS DECK IS THE HOME, and it is the half that is easy to miss: {6}{W}{W} puts each exiled card into its OWNER'S graveyard and creates a 2/2 Robot under YOUR control for each one, then sacrifices the Starcage. In a reanimator deck that is not a drawback — it loads your own yard (targets already reports 19-20 live reanimation targets) and converts the opponent's cheap board into your creatures. Grade it as a 3-mana one-sided sweeper with late upside, NOT as an engine: card advantage here is 1, the Starcage adds none, and 11 mana across two turns in a 25-land deck means the Robot half is a long-game bonus.
#~ note: Emeritus of Woe is the listed cut as the deck's only card flagged OFF its central themes (fit 16, the roster-lowest here) — its prepared trigger needs TWO OR MORE creatures to have died in a turn, and this build reanimates rather than sacrifices, so that gate is rarely on. It is also the only cut candidate that IMPROVES the curve (3.80 -> 3.77). What it costs, which the fit score cannot see: the sorcery half is an unconditional tutor. Two alternatives if that matters more in play — Judge Magister Gabranth and Stone Docent are both cut candidates AND among the five self-hits, so cutting either also makes the wipe more one-sided (5 -> 4). Do NOT cut Night Nurse (returns a permanent card that hit the yard this turn — she plays directly with the Starcage's own activation) or Undercover Skrull (any-color fixer holding up the {W}{W} reanimation engine).
