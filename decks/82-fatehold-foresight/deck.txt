#: name: Fatehold Foresight (UW surveil Jace)
#: format: Standard
#: arena: 82 Sphinx All-Seer, 3484759d-e8aa-4427-a355-88c16310ef84
#: colors: UW
#: archetype: Blue-heavy UW scry/surveil engine built from the Reality Fracture surveil pile. The resource is the scry/surveil EVENT: Matoya draws, Denzilore puts a +1/+1 counter on EVERY creature, and Proctor, Futurescribe and Saheeli turn on per creature or once a turn; Starfield Vocalist makes every entering trigger fire twice (Proctor surveils twice per creature; each Way empowers twice), and Planetarium of Wan Shi Tong turns the first surveil each turn into a free spell off the top. Echocasting Symposium, once resolved, copies a creature for free every turn. The Jace token every empower card makes (or loads) is the repeatable source — its −1 is a surveil every turn, Jace's Machinations adds a second activation on the opponent's turn — and Chandra, Chill's +1 is another. The Theorist draws on their draw step, so Lyra's three-draw Angel is reachable. Interaction is lean — Your Fate Ends Here, Prophesied End, Trapped in the Screen, Sheltered by Ghosts, Countersculpt, Ajani's −2 and the Jaces' minus abilities — and the deck leans on protection (Sygg, Kid Loki) and recursion (Proctor) as well. Distinct: shares ≤2 nonland cards with any roster deck; this is the UW Jace deck the Bant pile analysis left open (its "deck 2"), drafted from the surveil side.
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: B — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor A: interaction 8 (Your Fate Ends Here, Prophesied End, Trapped in the Screen, Sheltered by Ghosts, Countersculpt, The Theorist's −2, Jace, Reality Sculptor's −3, Ajani's −2; Loch Mare's tap-and-stun is not counted), one above the A line, and card advantage 11 that plays higher still — every empower card's draw is the Jace token's −3, in reminder text the role model strips. Protection reads 4, but one of those is Trapped in the Screen's ward, which guards the enchantment rather than a creature; the creature saves are Sheltered by Ghosts' ward {2} on the creature it enchants, Sygg's protection from every colour each turn and Kid Loki's hexproof on creatures that got a +1/+1 counter that turn. Held at B by two weaknesses, one more than the A band allows: a top-heavy curve (average mana value 3.33 with seven cards at 5–6, eleven early drops, on 24 lands — keepable 84%) and the mana — ten cards below 90% on curve (Ajani's {W}{W} lowest at 68%) on U 20 / W 14 — Theorist's Sanctum in for University Campus (2026-10-07) cost the fifteenth white source. Board power 53 against a roster median of 56, before tokens and counters, which that figure cannot see. Re-grade toward A after it has played.
