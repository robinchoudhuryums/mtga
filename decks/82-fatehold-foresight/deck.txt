#: name: Fatehold Foresight (UW surveil Jace)
#: format: Standard
#: colors: UW
#: archetype: Blue-heavy UW scry/surveil engine built from the Reality Fracture surveil pile. The resource is the scry/surveil EVENT: Matoya draws, Proft draws and grows, Denzilore puts a +1/+1 counter on EVERY creature, Diviner grows, and Proctor/Futurescribe/Prudent Fateseer turn on once a turn. The Jace token every empower card makes (or loads) is the repeatable source — its −1 is a surveil every turn, Jace's Machinations adds a second activation on the opponent's turn — and Chandra, Chill's +1 is another. The Theorist draws on their draw step, so Lyra's three-draw Angel is reachable. Interaction is blue tempo plus Erode: bounce-surveil instants that hit ANY nonland permanent, Plan for All Outcomes, two Countersculpts, Fatehold Charm. Distinct: shares ≤2 nonland cards with any roster deck; this is the UW Jace deck the Bant pile analysis left open (its "deck 2"), drafted from the surveil side.
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: B — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor A: interaction 12 (Erode, Yuriko's −X/−0, two Countersculpts, Fatehold Charm, Banishing Betrayal, Unauthorized Exit, Icy Reception, Infinite Coursework, Plan for All Outcomes, The Theorist's −2, Jace, Reality Sculptor's −3) and card advantage 8 that plays higher — every empower card's draw is the Jace token's −3, in reminder text the role model strips. Held at B by two real weaknesses, which is one more than the A band allows: ZERO protection for a deck whose engine is a handful of x/1–x/4 payoffs (Matoya, Proft, Denzilore, Proctor), and a small board (power 37, exactly the roster's p10 — Cadets, Illusions, Redwing and Denzilore's counters are tokens and counters, which that figure cannot see). The mana is a third, smaller cost: eight double-pip or two-colour cards cast on curve at 82–88% on U 20 / W 14. Re-grade toward A once it has played, or once a protection piece earns a slot.
#: protect: Matoya, Archon Elder; Proft, Consulting Detective; Denzilore Fatehold; Proctor of Potential; Jace's Machinations; Way of the Mind Sculptor; Way of the Cryomancer; Way of the Healer
#: notes: Drafted 2026-10-05 from .cycle/fra-surveil-pile-analysis.md. Mono-U, UW and UB were measured on the same blue core — all floor A; UW carries 8 scry/surveil payoffs against mono-U's 5 and UB's 3, at 8 cards under 90% on curve (worst Countersculpt) against mono-U's 0. UB was rejected on mana (Vraska's Final Mercy 51%, Ral 58%) and on the theme (its black cards are removal and walkers, not payoffs). Saheeli, Consul of Oversight is the one white payoff left out ({3}{W}{W} on 14 white sources). Every empower card's card advantage is the Jace token's −3, which sits in reminder text the role model strips, so `stats`/`tier` under-count card advantage here; `engines` has no scry/surveil pair, so the deck's real loop does not show there either. Weakest slots: Icy Reception, Infinite Coursework, Mindseeker Oculus, Protege's Awakening, Prudent Fateseer; bench: The Legend of Kuruk, Planetarium of Wan Shi Tong, Unwelcome Sprite. 2026-10-05 (owner's calls): Wan Shi Tong, Librarian and Semester Foreseer in for both Theorist's Proxy (empower 3 traded for a scaling flash flier and a 3/4 that surveils and re-prepares a Cadet), Yuriko in for one Fatehold Chronologist, Falcon, Winged Wonder in for one Sphinx of False Conclusions (Redwing surveils on every attack). Board power 34 → 37, interaction 11 → 12. Fatehold Annex is untapped once any planeswalker (the Jace token included) is out.
