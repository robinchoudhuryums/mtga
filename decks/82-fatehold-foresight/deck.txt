#: name: Fatehold Foresight (UW surveil Jace)
#: format: Standard
#: colors: UW
#: archetype: Blue-heavy UW scry/surveil engine built from the Reality Fracture surveil pile. The resource is the scry/surveil EVENT: Matoya draws, Proft draws and grows, Denzilore puts a +1/+1 counter on EVERY creature, Diviner grows, and Proctor, Gossip's Talent, Futurescribe and Saheeli turn on per creature or once a turn; Starfield Vocalist makes every entering trigger fire twice (Proctor and Gossip's Talent surveil twice per creature; each Way empowers twice). The Jace token every empower card makes (or loads) is the repeatable source — its −1 is a surveil every turn, Jace's Machinations adds a second activation on the opponent's turn — and Chandra, Chill's +1 is another. The Theorist draws on their draw step, so Lyra's three-draw Angel is reachable. Interaction is light by design since 2026-10-06 — Your Fate Ends Here, Countersculpt, Banishing Betrayal, Diviner's bounce spell and the Jaces' minus abilities — and the deck leans on protection instead (Sygg, Kid Loki, Skycoach's blink) and on recursion (Primary Research, Sphinx of Forgotten Lore, Proctor). Distinct: shares ≤2 nonland cards with any roster deck; this is the UW Jace deck the Bant pile analysis left open (its "deck 2"), drafted from the surveil side.
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: B — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor B, and B is the honest letter: interaction 6 (Your Fate Ends Here, Countersculpt, Banishing Betrayal, Diviner's bounce spell, The Theorist's −2, Jace, Reality Sculptor's −3) is one short of the A floor's 7, because the 2026-10-06 swaps traded five answers for engine and resilience. Card advantage 12 plays higher still — every empower card's draw is the Jace token's −3, in reminder text the role model strips — and protection is 2 (Sygg's protection from every colour each turn, Kid Loki's hexproof on creatures that got a +1/+1 counter that turn; Skycoach Conductor's instant blink is a third save the count cannot see). Weaknesses: few answers to a resolved threat, especially an artifact or enchantment, and the mana — eight double-pip or two-colour cards cast on curve at 82–88% on U 20 / W 14. The board is not the gap (power 52 against a roster median of 57, before tokens and counters, which that figure cannot see). One more interaction piece restores the A floor; re-grade after it has played.
#: protect: Matoya, Archon Elder; Proft, Consulting Detective; Denzilore Fatehold; Proctor of Potential; Jace's Machinations; Way of the Mind Sculptor; Way of the Cryomancer; Way of the Healer
