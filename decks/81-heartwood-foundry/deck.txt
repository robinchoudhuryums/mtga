#: name: Heartwood Foundry (RG artifact tokens + Dragon forge)
#: format: Standard
#: colors: RG
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: A — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor A on interaction 12 + card advantage 4: five burn/fight spells, Legion Extruder and Mouser Foundry's damage, Chandra Torch, Vivien Reid, and Ajani Unrelenting's −3, which spares every one of this deck's tokens. Weaknesses: card advantage is the thin axis (Spark Hunter's +2, Vivien's +1 and Ajani's −2 carry it); 7 of 12 nonbasic lands can enter tapped; and the forge is ONE card — without Draconic Visitor the tokens are mana and chump-blockers until Craterclaw or Pyrewood cashes them in. Re-grade after 20 matches.
#: archetype: RED-GREEN ARTIFACT TOKENS, drafted 2026-10-03 from the owner's Reality Fracture red-green(+blue) pile (.cycle/fra-rgu-pile-analysis.md), REPLACING the black-red walker deck that held this slot (git history keeps it). THE ENGINE: Reality Fracture's Heartwood is a red/green mana artifact, and this list makes artifact tokens from fourteen sources — Woodwork Prodigy re-prepares every upkeep (a Heartwood each turn), Eusocial Engineering makes a Robot per land, Chandra Spark Hunter a Vehicle per turn, Rocketeer Boostbuggy a Treasure per attack, plus Aerid Konstrari, Tenured Tethermage, Konstrari Improviser, Heartwood Crafter, Mutagen Man, Sandstorm Salvager, Legion Extruder, Mouser Foundry, Biotech Specialist and Galactic Wayfarer. Those tokens are mana, so the 6- and 7-drops (Pyrewood Gearhulk, Craterclaw Colossus, Savage Ventmaw, Ajani) arrive on time, and they are bodies for Craterclaw's +X-per-artifact and Puppetbeast's / Biotech's / Extruder's sacrifice outlets. THE FORGE: Draconic Visitor turns every artifact token into a 5/5 flying Dragon instead — Prodigy becomes a Dragon a turn, Engineering a Dragon per land — and the Dragon cards (Bloomvine Regent's life, Sarkhan, Encroaching Dragonstorm's rebuy, the Exhales' behold) pay for it. Its Dragons are NOT artifacts, so with Visitor out the artifact-count payoffs stop growing: it is a one-of finisher, not the plan. DISTINCT from 49 Big Draco (mono-red Dragons, 0 shared cards) and 59 Stampede (red-green attack-for-mana).
#: protect: Draconic Visitor; Woodwork Prodigy // Soul Tether; Aerid Konstrari; Eusocial Engineering
#: notes: Drafted 2026-10-03 in place of the black-red Detention Hall (owner's call: the pile was reworked into red-green). Built OPTIMAL per the Player Profile (4 Stomping Ground are the main craft; Reality Fracture cards are not yet ingested, so the library reads most of them as unowned). Cut from the measured scratch list to lower the curve (3.94 avg): Archive Arbiter, Kiora of Fire and Ashes, Craftwork Crusher, Sagu Wildling, Chandra Flameshaper. Owned additions from a maker survey: Legion Extruder, Mouser Foundry, Sarkhan Dragon Ascendant, Jolene Plundering Pugilist, Galactic Wayfarer. Temur (+Saheeli) was measured and rejected: every red-green gold card lost ~10 points on curve for one blue card.
