#: name: Heartwood Foundry (RG artifact tokens + Dragon forge)
#: format: Standard
#: colors: RG
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: A — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor A on interaction 11 + card advantage 4: four burn/fight spells, Legion Extruder's damage, Roxanne's 2-damage artifact token and Smaug's attack trigger, Chandra Torch, Vivien Reid, and Ajani Unrelenting's −3, which spares every one of this deck's tokens. Weaknesses: card advantage is the thin axis (Spark Hunter's +2, Vivien's +1 and Ajani's −2 carry it); 3 of 8 nonbasic lands can enter tapped (the Stomping Grounds went to basics 2026-10-04); and the forge is ONE card — without Draconic Visitor the tokens are mana and chump-blockers until Craterclaw or Pyrewood cashes them in. Re-grade after 20 matches.
#: archetype: RED-GREEN ARTIFACT TOKENS, drafted 2026-10-03 from the owner's Reality Fracture red-green(+blue) pile (.cycle/fra-rgu-pile-analysis.md), REPLACING the black-red walker deck that held this slot (git history keeps it). THE ENGINE: Reality Fracture's Heartwood is a red/green mana artifact, and this list makes artifact tokens from fourteen sources — Woodwork Prodigy re-prepares every upkeep (a Heartwood each turn), Eusocial Engineering makes a Robot per land, Chandra Spark Hunter a Vehicle per turn, Rocketeer Boostbuggy a Treasure per attack, plus Aerid Konstrari, Tenured Tethermage, Konstrari Improviser, Heartwood Crafter, Mutagen Man, Sandstorm Salvager, Legion Extruder, Seedship Agrarian (a Lander whenever it taps — attacking or crewing the Boostbuggy), Biotech Specialist, Terrapact Intimidator (two Landers or a 4/3 — the opponent's choice), and Roxanne Starfall Savant, whose tokens are artifacts AND who doubles every artifact token's mana; Smaug the Magnificent adds a Treasure every upkeep. Those tokens are mana, so the 6- and 7-drops (Pyrewood Gearhulk, Craterclaw Colossus, Raph & Mikey, Ajani) arrive on time, and they are bodies for Craterclaw's +X-per-artifact and Puppetbeast's / Biotech's / Extruder's sacrifice outlets. THE FORGE: Draconic Visitor turns every artifact token into a 5/5 flying Dragon instead — Prodigy becomes a Dragon a turn, Engineering a Dragon per land — and the Dragon cards (Bloomvine Regent's life, Sarkhan, Smaug's upkeep Treasure — itself a Dragon under Visitor — and the Exhales' behold) pay for it. Its Dragons are NOT artifacts, so with Visitor out the artifact-count payoffs stop growing: it is a one-of finisher, not the plan. DISTINCT from 49 Big Draco (mono-red Dragons, 0 shared cards) and 59 Stampede (red-green attack-for-mana).
