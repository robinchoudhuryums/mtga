Metrics floor A on interaction 15 + card advantage 6, the widest margin above the A line of any recent draft; the role model cannot see the Jace token's draws (reminder text) or Liliana, Dreadhorde General's draw-per-death beyond one count. Weaknesses: printed board power 23 counts no tokens, so the real board (4/4 Beasts, Cadets, Zombies, Kiora's Dragon) arrives a turn behind the walkers, and a fast aggro start can kill walkers before the loop runs; four 6-drops (Ajani, Liliana DG, Kiora, Overwrite) make the top heavy; protection 1 — the engine pieces (Necromancer, Deathbringer, Sothera) are enchantments with no way to defend them. Re-grade after 20 matches.
#: archetype: RAKDOS PLANESWALKER-ARISTOCRATS, drafted 2026-10-03 from the Reality Fracture blue/black/red pile (.cycle/fra-bur-pile-analysis.md). THE LOOP: every loyalty activation makes a 2/2 Cadet (Ajani Unrelenting); Way of the Deathbringer gives every walker "−2: sacrifice a creature → 4/4 trample Beast", so the Cadet becomes a Beast; every death puts +1 loyalty on EVERY walker (Way of the Necromancer), draws (Liliana, Dreadhorde General), drains (Massacre Girl, Most Wanted; Funeral Room) and makes the opponent exile a creature (Sothera). Sanctum Lurker keeps walkers alive at 0 loyalty and gives each "+2: 1 damage to each opponent, gain 1 life", so the big minuses (Deathbringer −2, Warlord −4) can be spent freely. Way of the Pyromancer's +1 makes red mana. The Ways put their abilities on EVERY walker, the Jace token included, so a modest walker (The Aetherspark, Chandra Spark Hunter) is still a carrier for all four. DISTINCT from 60 Redline (black-red max-speed aggro) and 55 Mardu Waves (attack-trigger tokens): the win here is walker loyalty and repeated edicts, not combat speed.
#: protect: Way of the Necromancer; Way of the Deathbringer; Ajani Unrelenting; Sothera, the Supervoid; Sanctum Lurker
#: notes: Drafted 2026-10-03. Built OPTIMAL per the Player Profile: the Reality Fracture cards are not yet ingested, so the library reads most of them as unowned; the owner holds the pile. Lands are the optimal black-red set (4 Blood Crypt, 4 Stingerquill Annex — untapped with a walker out) rather than gated on ownership. Drafting swaps (re-screen of the rejected pile): Nocturnal Hunger for Murder (the same instant kill at {2}{B} instead of {1}{B}{B}); The Aetherspark for Tezzeret, Cruel Captain (both are cheap colourless Way-ability carriers, but Tezzeret's own abilities find nothing here while Aetherspark protects itself and its −5 draws two). Left out and why: Tinybones (only Ral −1 / Garruk −3 make discards), Chandra Flameshaper (a 7-drop on an already heavy top), Meathook Massacre II ({B}{B}{B}{B}), Midnight Reaper (draws on NONTOKEN deaths only — the fodder here is tokens), Draconic Visitor (Chandra Spark Hunter's Vehicle is the only artifact token).
