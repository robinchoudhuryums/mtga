#: archetype: Equipment and Treasures that is frequently lethal from a stalled board, and it
#: archetype: does not care whether any of them can attack. Radiant Lotus converts a Treasure
#: archetype: pile into the mana to get there early; Push the Limit returns every Vehicle in the
#: archetype: graveyard with haste for one alpha strike.
#: archetype: DISTINCTNESS from its own parent: 48 wins by making a wide board bigger and needs
