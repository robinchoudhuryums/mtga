#: archetype: is not a creature at all (that is the 20+ line, effectively unreachable), so it

#: archetype: DISTINCTNESS: the poison plan moved out to variant 26a, which is built on
#: archetype: Virulent Silencer and many cheap evasive NONTOKEN bodies. This deck keeps the
#: archetype: Silencer as a free alternate win (one owned copy serves both decks) but is
#: archetype: built the opposite way: few enormous bodies instead of many small evasive ones.
