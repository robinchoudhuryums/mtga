#~ -Loot, Exuberant Explorer | +The Earth Crystal | OWNED, and a real card: "Green spells you cast cost {1} less" plus counter-doubling (live with Twitching Doll, Tender Wildguide's Offspring and Famished Worldsire's devour counters). Parked, not rejected. THE PROBLEM IS THAT THIS DECK HAS NO FILLER SLOT: every candidate cut is either a cheap accelerant (curve), a creature (the currency that Craterhoof's X, Enduring Vitality and Three Tree City all read), or a cheat engine. Measured against five cuts; the cheapest is this one at avg MV 3.11 -> 3.14, early drops held at 21, creatures 26 -> 25. Cutting a LLANOWAR ELVES instead was the WORST option tried (avg MV 3.19, early 20, creatures 25) — it is the deck's only 1-mana accelerant and it is also a Druid and a creature, so it pays into all three counters where the Crystal pays into none. The honest cost of this swap is that Loot is itself a CHEAT ENGINE ("{4}{G}{G}, {T}: look at the top six, put a creature with mana value <= your land count onto the battlefield"), and its extra land drop is what gets you to the land count that turns it on — so this trades a second Craterhoof route for a cost reducer. Take it only if the deck plays out as mana-starved rather than threat-starved.
#~ -Clash of the Eikons | +Earth's Mightiest Heroes | OWNED, and the biggest cheat effect in green: teamwork 5 (tap creatures with total power 5+), reveal eight, and put ANY NUMBER of creature cards from among them onto the battlefield. In a deck that goes wide and then reads creature count three ways, resolving it with teamwork is often three or four bodies at once — and Craterhoof's X counts every one. Parked because it MEASURED as a curve cost in every configuration: against Clash of the Eikons it took interaction 6 -> 5, avg MV 3.11 -> 3.25 and early drops 21 -> 20; against Great Forest Druid, avg MV -> 3.22 with a creature lost; adding it alongside Sledge-Class Seedship read 3.28 / 19 early. This deck's whole advantage is a 3.11 curve with 21 early drops, and it already runs TWO cheat engines (Michelangelo, Loot). Take this only if the deck plays out as top-heavy-but-slow rather than fast-but-shallow. The rest of the cheat package went to 50a, which is slow enough to pay for it: Ojer Kaslem, Kona Rescue Beastie and Web of Life and Destiny.
