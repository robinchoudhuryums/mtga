#: name: Darkforce Inversion

#: archetype: Orzhov (W/B) LIFEGAIN-DRAIN control on a blink shell. Life is the
#: archetype: resource. Lifelink bodies and gain-triggers push your total up, drain
#: archetype: payoffs convert each gain into life LOSS for the opponent, and Mister
#: archetype: Negative exchanges the two totals. A blink is a RE-ENTRY, so flickering him
#: archetype: TWICE draws the whole gap and puts both totals back (swap, draw, swap back);
#: archetype: Another Round does it alone at X=1.
#: archetype: THE KILL, added 2026-09-18, is a two-card loop: Bloodthirsty Conqueror
#: archetype: ("whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much") plus either
#: archetype: Starscape Cleric or Enduring Tenacity ("whenever you gain life, they lose
#: archetype: that much") loops until the opponent is dead. The lifegain side runs fifteen
#: archetype: enablers, three of them lands, so a land drop can start it.
#: archetype: LIFE IS ALSO SPENT, not just banked: Inside Information buys mana with it,
#: archetype: Treasure Dredger buys a Treasure, Foolish Fate and Poisoner's Apprentice pay
#: archetype: off having gained it. Spending down before a swap is not a cost — the swap
#: archetype: refunds it out of the opponent's total and craters theirs in the same motion.
#: archetype: DISTINCTNESS vs 20 Honor Among Thieves, the roster's other Orzhov deck:
#: archetype: 20 is a lifegain-drain AGGRO deck — evasive Ninjas on a clock, using life as
#: archetype: a Kingpin win-con. This one converts life into CARDS and MANA and closes with
#: archetype: the loop; it runs no Ninjas and no sneak package, and its creature base is
#: archetype: chosen for ETB text rather than evasion.
#: tier: B — Orzhov lifegain-drain control, deliberately held ONE BAND UNDER a metrics
#: tier: floor of A. Rebuilt 2026-09-18 (ten swaps) after the list was read as weak
#: tier: despite clearing the floor: the surveil package was cut for real top-end and a
#: tier: combo kill. Live vector: interaction 9 over a 3.36 curve, card advantage 11,
#: tier: protection 2. Board power went 41 to 58, from rank 18 of 112 on the roster to
#: tier: about its median — that axis is the one the floor cannot see and the one that
#: tier: made the old list read weak.
#: tier: WHAT HOLDS IT AT B, against a floor that says A:
#: tier: 1. THE CURVE. 3.36 against 24 lands, and the figure UNDER-reports: three X-cost
#: tier:    cards book at MV 1. Treasure Dredger is the only acceleration. A 25th land

#: notes: WHY THE SWAP WORKS — Mister Negative's ETB is a "may", and the draw clause
#: notes: fires only when YOUR life was higher. Two modes, and they are alternatives, not

#: notes: already 11, and closing is the thing it is short of.
#: notes: SO PAYING LIFE IS NOT A DRAWBACK HERE. Inside Information buys mana at mana

#~ +Painful Quandary | -Stroke of Midnight | PRO-synergy here in a way its black flavour hides: it is the one taxer on the list that is NOT symmetric (it triggers only on OPPONENTS' spells and costs you nothing), and pushing their total DOWN widens the exact gap Mister Negative converts into cards. Held out of the 60 only on curve — MV 5 against a 3.36 average, and it does nothing on an empty board. The 2026-09-18 rebuild raised the curve, so this is now a harder sell, not an easier one. Owned.

#~ +Prayer of Binding | -Banishing Light | The FLASH half of the answer Banishing Light already provides — it exiles a nonland permanent an opponent controls until it leaves, plus 2 life, held up on the opponent's turn instead of telegraphed on yours (and its target is "up to one", so it can be flashed in blank for the 2 life when nothing is worth hitting). In a control deck that wants to keep mana open that is the better card; it costs {3}{W} against {2}{W}, which is why it is a flex and not the maindeck pick. Banishing Light was added 2026-09-17 and has not been played, so decide this after games rather than before. Owned.
#~ note: SUCCESSION for Another Round (rotates ~2026). Nothing in Standard reproduces it — a regex sweep of the pool for repeated-blink text ("repeat this process", "do this again") returns five cards, of which only Another Round blinks your own board more than once for one spell. So there is no swap to plan; the plan is structural. When it rotates, the one-card double re-entry of Mister Negative (swap, draw the gap, swap back) becomes a two-card or two-turn line, carried by Airbender Ascension's end-step blink plus any one-shot blink. Re-check this note after each set release rather than treating it as settled.
