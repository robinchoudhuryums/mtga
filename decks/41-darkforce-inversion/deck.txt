#~ +Painful Quandary | -Stroke of Midnight | PRO-synergy here in a way its black flavour hides: it is the one taxer on the list that is NOT symmetric (it triggers only on OPPONENTS' spells and costs you nothing), and pushing their total DOWN widens the exact gap Mister Negative converts into cards. Held out of the 60 only on curve — MV 5 against a 2.92 average, and it does nothing on an empty board. Owned.
#~ +The Mind Stone | -Visions of Villainy | The ONLY way to re-trigger a noncreature ETB in this deck: all six blink spells read "exile target CREATURE you control", while the Mind Stone's harnessed ability blinks any nonland permanent — so it is what unlocks Airbender Ascension, Stolen Stark Tech, Eerie Gravestone and Foraging Wickermaw. Indestructible, so it survives the removal that answers the rest of the engine. Slow: {5}{W} plus a tap to harness, and a plain mana rock before that. RETARGETED 2026-09-17 — this line used to propose cutting Airbender Ascension, which is one of the four permanents the Mind Stone exists to re-trigger. Owned.
#~ +The Seriema | -Personify | The SECOND tutor for the marquee payoff: "search your library for a legendary creature card, reveal it, put it into your hand" finds Mister Negative, the same way Seek the Heart does, and doubling that search is the cheapest fix for the reliability point in `#: tier:`. Its rider — "other tapped legendary creatures you control have indestructible" — is NARROW here and should not be read as the protection this deck is short on: Mister Negative has vigilance, so he is rarely tapped at all, and the grant only really applies to a creature you tapped to Station this. Costs {1}{W}{W} against Personify's {1}{W}, and it is not a blink target (the deck's six blink spells all read "creature you control"). Owned.
#~ +Anchovy & Banana Pizza | -Getaway Glamer | Unconditional "when this artifact enters, destroy target creature" against Getaway Glamer's Spree mode, which only kills "if no other creature has greater power" — i.e. it whiffs on exactly the threat you most need dead. Its sacrifice mode gains 3 life, which turns ON Poisoner's Apprentice's Infusion clause ("if you gained life this turn") in a deck where that gate is otherwise carried by the lifelink bodies alone. The cost is real: {2}{B}{B} against {W}, on a 2.92 curve, and it is an artifact rather than a creature, so only The Mind Stone can re-trigger it. Owned.
#~ +Prayer of Binding | -Banishing Light | The FLASH half of the answer Banishing Light already provides — it exiles a nonland permanent an opponent controls until it leaves, plus 2 life, held up on the opponent's turn instead of telegraphed on yours (and its target is "up to one", so it can be flashed in blank for the 2 life when nothing is worth hitting). In a control deck that wants to keep mana open that is the better card; it costs {3}{W} against {2}{W}, which is why it is a flex and not the maindeck pick. Banishing Light was added 2026-09-17 and has not been played, so decide this after games rather than before. Owned.
#~ note: SUCCESSION for Another Round (rotates ~2026). Nothing in Standard reproduces it — a regex sweep of the pool for repeated-blink text ("repeat this process", "do this again") returns five cards, of which only Another Round blinks your own board more than once for one spell. So there is no swap to plan; the plan is structural. When it rotates, the one-card double re-entry of Mister Negative (swap, draw the gap, swap back) becomes a two-card or two-turn line, carried by Airbender Ascension's end-step blink plus any one-shot blink. Re-check this note after each set release rather than treating it as settled.
