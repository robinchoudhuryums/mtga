#: name: Blood Price — Orzhov Aristocrats
#: format: Standard
#: colors: WB
#: plan: midrange
#: archetype: 42 variant — Orzhov (W/B) ARISTOCRATS. Same Orzhov attrition shell as the
#: archetype: core, with the engine swapped out: the core buys cards with LIFE and hands
#: archetype: the debt over once with Mister Negative; this build spends CREATURES and
#: archetype: never runs Negative at all. Cheap recurring token makers (Lunar
#: archetype: Convocation's Bats, Bitterbloom Bearer's Faeries, Fountainport's Fish,
#: archetype: Scavenger's Talent's Food, the dies-into-a-body creatures) feed five
#: archetype: sacrifice outlets, and every death is taxed by a drain or a draw: Funeral
#: archetype: Room and Vengeful Bloodwitch drain 1 and gain 1 per creature, Arnyn drains
#: archetype: 2 for a 1-power token, Al Bhed Salvagers counts artifacts too (so a
#: archetype: sacrificed Clue, Food or Treasure drains), Agent Venom and Liliana turn
#: archetype: nontoken deaths into cards, and Judge Magister Gabranth / Sandbender
#: archetype: Scavengers grow on every one.
#: archetype: THE OUTLETS ARE THE POINT. The core deck fielded death triggers like
#: archetype: Vengeful Bloodwitch with NOTHING to feed them on demand, so they only fired
#: archetype: when the opponent obliged. Baron Bertram Graywater (a token engine that is also a
#: archetype: sacrifice-for-draw outlet), Vampiric Rites and Ahriman are what convert those
#: archetype: passive triggers into an engine you control. Umbral Collar Zealot was CUT for
#: archetype: Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim, a free-ish outlet that also blocks on deathtouch;
#: archetype: Kingpin's Enforcers was CUT for Tragedy Feaster.
#: archetype: EIRDU IS THE MULTIPLIER: once transformed, every other nontoken creature
#: archetype: has persist, so each one dies TWICE — two Funeral Room drains, two Bloodwitch
#: archetype: triggers, two Agent Venom draws — and comes back to be sacrificed again.
#: archetype: DISTINCTNESS vs deck 8, the roster's other aristocrats deck: that one is
#: archetype: RAKDOS (B/R with green) and closes with red reach and big black finishers.
#: archetype: This is two-color Orzhov and wins on the LIFEGAIN-DRAIN axis rather than
#: archetype: burn — every trigger here gains you life as it costs them life, which is a
#: archetype: race-winning posture that deck does not have. `similar` confirms it: only
#: archetype: THREE nonland cards are shared. vs the core 42: opposite
#: archetype: resource. 42 spends life and holds creatures; this spends creatures and
#: archetype: gains life.
#: tier: B — Orzhov aristocrats, PROVISIONAL (unplayed brew). The claimed letter sits AT
#: tier: the metrics floor rather than one band above it. Interaction fell 7 -> 5 when
#: tier: Harsh Annotation and Sephiroth's Intervention became Fix What's Broken and
#: tier: Starseer Mentor, and Social Snub (over Anafenza) has brought it back to 6 — an
#: tier: edict that COPIES itself, so it answers two creatures and drains twice while you
#: tier: feed it a spare token. `cuts` also flags Liliana's -4 double edict and Jecht's
#: tier: sacrifice trigger as likely under-reads, so the true figure is nearer 8; that is
#: tier: a reason to verify, not a reason to claim the number.
#: tier: BE PRECISE ABOUT THE FLOOR, because "restore the interaction" was not the whole
#: tier: fix: the A floor wants interaction >=5 AND interaction+card-advantage >=7. At
#: tier: interaction 4 and card advantage 1 the sum is 5, so the floor is B and TWO more
#: tier: points across the two axes flip it. Card advantage is the easier axis to move here —
#: tier: see risk 1 below for why the measured 1 is defensible but beatable.
#: tier: THE MEASURED 4 UNDER-READS THIS DECK and `tier` says so itself, printing 4 +2?
#: tier: and naming both misses. Liliana's -4 (each player sacrifices two creatures) and
#: tier: Jecht's chapter III (each OPPONENT sacrifices two) are unambiguous interaction the
#: tier: classifier cannot see, because loyalty abilities and Saga chapters are not in its
#: tier: patterns; Arnyn's deathtouch is soft interaction that also feeds the death triggers
#: tier: when it trades. Call the real figure 6. Heartless Act was CUT on exactly that
#: tier: reasoning — it was the one CONDITIONAL answer ("creature with no counters"), and
#: tier: what the uncounted pieces cannot replace is a cheap unconditional answer on demand,
#: tier: which is why Hero's Downfall and Erode stay.
#: tier: Held at B by three further things the floor cannot see:
#: tier: 1. MEASURED CARD ADVANTAGE IS ONE, and that number is defensible rather than a
#: tier:    classifier miss — Treacherous Greed's draw-three is the single card that
#: tier:    clears the bar. Vampiric Rites, Ahriman, Baron Bertram
#: tier:    and Agent Venom each draw exactly ONE card, which the role model excludes as
#: tier:    card-neutral — and in the abstract it is right, because sacrificing a CARD to
#: tier:    draw a card is a wash. It is only advantage when the fodder is a TOKEN, which
#: tier:    is why the token base is built to recur. So the deck's card advantage is real
#: tier:    but CONDITIONAL on having a token engine online, and a metric cannot see that.
#: tier: 2. AN ARISTOCRATS DECK NEEDS TWO HALVES AT ONCE — an outlet AND a payoff on the
#: tier:    battlefield — and a singleton build draws one half far more often than both.
#: tier:    Five outlets against six drain payoffs is the virtual-redundancy answer, but
#: tier:    virtual copies are not 4-ofs, and protection is 1 (Restoration Magic) against
#: tier:    three `#: protect:` build-arounds.
#: tier: 3. THE CLOCK IS SLOW AND WIPE-FRAGILE. Drain 1 per death is a turn-12 kill
#: tier:    without Eirdu, and a board wipe is strictly worse for this deck than for the
#: tier:    core — it takes the fodder and the payoffs in the same breath, where the core
#: tier:    42 survives a wipe on its card engine.
#: tier: NOTE ON THE CURVE: this deck is what surfaced the split-cost bug. Funeral Room
#: tier: is a Room, and its stored cost is the MERGED `{2}{B} // {6}{B}{B}` at MV 11, so
#: tier: `stats` read the curve as 2.8 and `consistency` read the card as a {B}{B}{B}
#: tier: turn-5 play — when the door this deck casts is `{2}{B}`, one black pip on turn
#: tier: 3. `parse_pips` and `load_mana` now read the FRONT face, so the reported 2.57
#: tier: is the real number and Funeral Room no longer shows up as a castability risk.
#: tier: PATH TO A: Eirdu is the single biggest lever — persist turns a 12-turn drain
#: tier: into a 6-turn one — followed by a second protection effect for the payoff
#: tier: enchantments.
#: protect: Funeral Room // Awakening Hall; Baron Bertram Graywater; Scavenger's Talent
#: notes: WHAT WAS CUT FROM THE CORE AND WHY. Mister Negative, Lunar Convocation's
#: notes: pay-2-life-draw mode, Phyrexian Arena, Visions of Villainy, Cruel Truths,
#: notes: Circle of Power and Decode Transmissions are all gone — that pay-life card
#: notes: engine IS the core deck, and keeping it would make this a 55-card overlap with
#: notes: a sacrifice sub-theme rather than a distinct build. Lunar Convocation itself
#: notes: STAYS, but purely as a Bat factory: the {1}{B} pay-2-life draw is a mode you
#: notes: mostly do not use here.
#: notes: FODDER QUALITY MATTERS MORE THAN FODDER COUNT. The token makers are chosen to
#: notes: be RECURRING (Lunar Convocation every end step, Bitterbloom Bearer every
#: notes: upkeep, Fountainport on demand, Scavenger's Talent once per turn, Treasure
#: notes: Dredger and Krumar Initiate on demand, Midnight Snack every turn you attacked,
#: notes: Krovod Haunch into two Dogs when sacrificed), because a one-shot token is one
#: notes: sacrifice and a recurring one is an engine. That principle drove every fodder
#: notes: cut: Infestation Sage, Dwarven Castle Guard, Resolute Reinforcements and Agents
#: notes: of HYDRA were all self-replacing but ONE-SHOT, and each replacement makes a body
#: notes: repeatedly. The deck now runs NO one-shot fodder at all.
#: notes: WHY ANAFENZA WAS CUT even though `cuts` rated her fit 40 / power 6, near the
#: notes: top of the deck: her trigger is "whenever another NONTOKEN creature you control
#: notes: dies", and the fodder base is now almost entirely TOKENS. She rewarded exactly
#: notes: the deaths this deck stopped having once every one-shot body became a recurring
#: notes: token generator. A fit score cannot see that the fodder changed underneath it —
#: notes: this is the grade-against-THIS-deck's-engine rule cutting the other way.
#: notes: WATCH THE ARTIFACT CLAUSES. Al Bhed Salvagers, Ahriman, Umbral Collar Zealot
#: notes: and Kingpin's Enforcers all read "creature OR artifact", so Clue tokens
#: notes: (Obsessive Pursuit), Food (Scavenger's Talent) and Treasure (Fountainport) are
#: notes: fodder too — that is a meaningful amount of extra fuel the creature count hides.
#: notes: Fountainport is doing three jobs: a land, a token maker, and a sacrifice outlet
#: notes: that draws. It is the most efficient slot in the deck.
#: notes: Craft targets: Eirdu, Carrier of Dawn (M), Agent Venom (R), Baron Bertram
#: notes: Graywater (U), Al Bhed Salvagers (U), Restoration Magic (U). Else all owned.
