#: protect: Mister Negative; Lunar Convocation; Phyrexian Arena
#: notes: HOW THE REFUND WORKS — exchanging totals only draws cards when YOUR life was
#: notes: higher, which is not this deck's situation. Here the swap is the good half:
#: notes: you are low, they are high, so you take their total and drop them to yours.
#: notes: Zero cards, a huge swing, and every point spent on draw comes back at once.
#: notes: RELIABILITY, stated honestly: one copy of a 7-drop is ~22% to be seen by turn

#~ +Grave Researcher | -Snarling Gorehound | Reanimation that COSTS LIFE — pure thesis — and it steals from ANY graveyard, so every creature the removal suite killed is a target. Upkeep surveil 1 also digs. The tax is that "prepared" needs three CREATURE cards in your own yard, so it is a mid-game card in a deck with no self-mill. Cut candidates, weakest first: Snarling Gorehound (a 1-drop whose surveil trigger needs power-2-or-less creatures ENTERING, which this build does less of after the swaps), Infestation Sage (a 1/1 that replaces itself but feeds no sacrifice outlet here), Callous Inspector. Owned.
#~ +Stargaze | -Infestation Sage | Pay X life, look at 2X, keep X — scalable draw at exactly the rate the deck wants, and the discarded half stocks the yard for Grave Researcher above. Cut candidates, weakest first: Infestation Sage, Callous Inspector (1 damage on death is the smallest effect in the deck), Black Widow, Super Spy (its mill-and-exile payoff wants combat damage this deck rarely deals). Do NOT cut removal for either of these — interaction is 8 after the swaps, down from 13. Owned.
#~ +Silverquill Charm | -Hour of Defeat | Two-mana modal instant: two counters, exile a power-2-or-less creature, or drain 3 and gain 3. Never dead, and cheaper than the four-drop removal it would replace. Craft (U).
#~ +Exsanguinate | -Erode | {X}{B}{B} scalable drain-and-gain — the finisher the deck's PATH TO A asks for. Caveat that keeps it in flex rather than the 60: it pushes THEIR total down, which shrinks the Mister Negative refund, so it is plan-B support that mildly undercuts plan A. Craft (U).
#~ +Starseer Mentor | -Hour of Defeat | "Gained OR LOST life this turn" is met every turn here without trying, so it is a repeatable lose-3-or-sacrifice-or-discard clock on a flier. Held out on curve: MV 5 against a 2.37 average. Craft (U).
#~ +The Spot's Portal | -Harsh Annotation | Instant-speed bottom-of-library removal, which beats exile against recursion, and the 2-life rider is on-thesis (waived while you control a Villain — Agents of HYDRA makes one). An UPGRADE OVER an existing removal spell, not an add. Craft (U).
#~ +Foolish Fate | -Harsh Annotation | Instant removal with an Infusion rider that drains 3 if you gained life this turn, which this deck does constantly. Same note as above: swap for a removal spell, do not add. Craft (U).
