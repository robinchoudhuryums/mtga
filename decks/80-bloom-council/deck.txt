#: name: Bloom Council (GW lifegain superfriends)
#: format: Standard
#: colors: GW
#: archetype: GW lifegain superfriends built from the Reality Fracture pile. The plan is a TWO-WAY loop: every life gain puts loyalty on every planeswalker (Way of the Mentor; Ajani Resolute on himself), and every loyalty activation gains a life (Way of the Paradox). Liliana the Faultless and Greenhouse Propagator gain life for EACH creature or token that enters, so every token is two life gains and so two loyalty on every walker — and Elspeth, Storm Slayer and Exalted Sunborn double the tokens. The Ways give every walker (the Jace token their own empower creates, included) extra loyalty abilities to spend it on. Distinct from deck 46 Lightwing (growing lifelink fliers): this deck wins through planeswalkers, and shares ≤3 nonland cards with any roster deck.
#: plan: midrange
#: tier: A — PROVISIONAL (untested brew). Metrics floor A: interaction 8 (Erode, Bite Down, Compel Brutality, Vigorbloom Charm, Your Fate Ends Here, Gideon's Memorial, plus Elspeth's and Ajani, Outland Chaperone's minus abilities) and card advantage that reads 3 but plays higher — the Jace token's draw from every empower sits in reminder text the role model strips. Exactly on the A line, so one interaction cut drops it. Weaknesses: the GG Garruk on 11 green sources, the WW three-drop Ajanis on 15 white, and a list of singletons with no redundancy for the two Ways the loop needs. Re-grade after 20 matches.
#: protect: Way of the Mentor; Way of the Paradox; Liliana the Faultless; Greenhouse Propagator; Ajani Resolute; Elspeth, Storm Slayer; Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion; Michelangelo, Weirdness to 11
#: notes: Drafted 2026-10-03 from the FRA pile (.cycle/fra-bant-pile-analysis.md). 3-colour Bant was measured and rejected on MANA (33 of 36 spells under 90% on curve, five tapped duals); mono-W is ~10 cards short. Vivien Reid was the GG walker cut (owner's call) — Garruk stays for his Beast/draw synergy with the token doublers. Astelli Reclaimer, Rinoa and Loyal Tutor were the last cuts. 2026-10-03: Solarium Sentry in for Tam's Resistance (owner's call, after `screen` rated it KEY) — a 2-mana 3/3 for a deck short on early drops, and its life gain on the OPPONENT's turn still puts loyalty on every walker through Way of the Mentor; the trade gives up Tam's empower 4. The empower cards' card draw (the Jace token's −3) sits in reminder text, so `stats`/`tier` under-count card advantage here. 2026-10-03: Verdant Kraken in for Repurposed Enforcer (owner's call, to add top end) — every upkeep, yours AND theirs, it makes a creature that is also a land, so each token fires Liliana/Greenhouse (life → loyalty via Mentor) and Koth's landfall, doubled by Elspeth/Sunborn, and ramps. {G}{G}{G} at MV 7 is ~51% to have the green by T7 on 12 sources; Kwia's Lotus, Garruk's +2 and a creature-cheat effect are the routes in.
