#: archetype: repeatable cantrip; Cloudblazer draws four; the gain-1 lands cantrip), and
#: archetype: hoards the result behind Ms. Marvel / Vnwxt / The Ten Rings, whose
#: archetype: no-maximum-hand-size clauses are what keep the hand deep enough to absorb
#: archetype: the Grimoire's discards. Ms. Marvel's power equals cards in hand, so the
