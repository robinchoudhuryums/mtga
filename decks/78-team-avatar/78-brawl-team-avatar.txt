#: archetype: Brawl conversion of the Bant Ally trigger-doubling deck (deck 78). The
#: archetype: Standard list was already 60-card singleton and built AROUND Katara, so she
#: archetype: is the natural commander: every Ally's triggered ability fires twice while
#: archetype: she is out, and from the command zone she is always available. The plan is
#: archetype: unchanged — Allies entering, South Pole Voyager / Avatar Enthusiasts / Haru
#: archetype: paying off each entry, with Starfield Vocalist, Elspeth and Doubling Season
#: archetype: multiplying the same events Katara doubles.
#: tier: B — PROVISIONAL, AT its B metrics floor, the same letter as deck 78, because the
#: tier: two lists differ by one card and the floor did not move: interaction 6 (Prayer of
#: tier: Binding replaces Jet's one answer), card advantage 3, protection 3, avg MV 3.39.
#: tier: PROVISIONAL because no Brawl game has been played with it. Deck 78's record comes
#: tier: from Bo1 Standard, so it says nothing about this format. The format argues both ways.
#: tier: UP: the engine is always in the command zone, and slower 1v1 singleton games
#: tier: give a four-multiplier board more time to assemble.
#: tier: DOWN: every opposing removal spell has a guaranteed target, and there is still no
#: tier: sweeper. Re-grade after games, not before.
#: protect: Katara, the Fearless; South Pole Voyager; Avatar Enthusiasts; Haru, Hidden Talent; Suki, Kyoshi Warrior
#: notes: ONE COLOUR-IDENTITY SWAP, and it is the only card that needed to change: Jet,
#: notes: Freedom Fighter is {2}{R/W}{R/W}{R/W}. Paid all-white he is castable in deck 78
#: notes: (G-58), but a hybrid symbol still counts toward colour identity, so the {R/W}
#: notes: pips make him R and illegal under a G/W/U commander. He is out for Prayer of
#: notes: Binding ({3}{W}, owned).
#: notes: WHY PRAYER OF BINDING. No other G/W/U Ally removal exists in the pool —
#: notes: owned or craftable — beyond Earth Kingdom Jailer and Ty Lee, which the deck already
#: notes: runs. Among the non-Ally answers it is the one this list wants, for four reasons.
#: notes: (1) It keeps interaction level: Jet's ETB was one targeted creature answer, and
#: notes: Prayer is one exile. (2) It covers a gap Jet did not: it has flash and can
#: notes: exile any nonland permanent, and the Standard list has only two answers to a
#: notes: noncreature permanent. (3) Its enters trigger is doubled by
#: notes: Starfield Vocalist ("If a permanent entering the battlefield causes a triggered
#: notes: ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an
#: notes: additional time"), so with her out it is TWO exiles and 4 life. (4) The life gain
#: notes: is the first rung of Belladonna Took's ladder. Katara does not double it; it is
#: notes: not an Ally.
#: notes: WHAT JET TOOK WITH HIM. He was an Ally, so under Katara his ETB fired twice, and
#: notes: his death trigger spread counters. Prayer is not an Ally and adds no body. The
#: notes: Ally count drops by one, and so does the fodder for South Pole Voyager and
#: notes: Avatar Enthusiasts.
#: notes: Jet's damage also scaled with the board. Prayer's exile is fixed but unbounded
#: notes: in what it hits.
#: notes: Prayer can be undone: removing the enchantment returns the card. That risk is
#: notes: small in Bo1 Brawl.
#: notes: Screened and passed over (all tangential to the engine): Stasis Snare, Liminal
#: notes: Hold (Prayer is Liminal Hold plus flash), Oko, Vivien Reid, Ajani Outland
#: notes: Chaperone, Season of the Burrow, Exclusion Mage, Wistfulness, Hardlight
#: notes: Containment, Parting Gust, Crib Swap and Season of Gathering.
#: notes: WHAT THE FORMAT CHANGES. The single point of failure that deck 78's tier block
#: notes: worries about (the engine is one legendary 3/3) is softened, not removed. She can
#: notes: be recast from the command zone, but each recast costs {2} more. Her turn-three
#: notes: castability is unchanged ({G}{W}{U} against the same 24 lands). Being in the
#: notes: command zone guarantees she is available; it does not make her castable earlier.
#: notes: Protection is 3 in this list. It matters more here than in the Standard deck,
#: notes: because every answer the opponent draws has a guaranteed target.
