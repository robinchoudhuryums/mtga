#~ -Invasion Tactics | +Katara, Water Tribe's Hope | SIDEGRADE (owned): Human Warrior Ally, vigilance, ETB Ally token (doubled by BOTH Katara the Fearless and Starfield), and waterbend {X} is a real finisher on a ten-token board -- creatures get BASE power/toughness X/X, so existing +1/+1 counters still stack on top, and the tap-to-help clause lets vigilance bodies attack AND pay. MV 5 for MV 5, so the curve does not move; it costs card advantage 4 -> 3 (Invasion Tactics draws whenever Allies connect, and Starfield doubles its +2/+2 ETB to +4/+4).
#~ note: Katara, Water Tribe's Hope is PARKED, not rejected, and the number to re-check is the manabase. Measured 2026-09-01: {2}{W}{U}{U} against 8 blue sources is 43% on curve at turn five -- it would become the deck's worst castability row, below Katara the Fearless at 51%, which is the row the tier block already names as what holds this deck at B. Great Divide Guide (every land AND Ally taps for any colour) and Dazzling Theater (convoke pays a coloured pip off a blue body) both rescue her, but P(drawing either by turn five) is only 33.6%, before either resolves or survives. She comes in when blue sources go up -- roughly 18 are needed for a 90% double pip -- not when a slot opens.
#~ -Kyoshi Warriors | +Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards | SIDEGRADE (owned): {3}{U} reach body that draws whenever one or more tokens you control enter. He was CUT 2026-09-01 for Celebrate the Mountain-king, which took interaction 7 -> 8 at the cost of card advantage 4 -> 3; he is the way to buy that draw back if the axis reads too thin in play. Cut listed as Kyoshi Warriors because both are MV 4 and the 4-slot is the curve's fat point.
#~ note: TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE BRINGING MISTER FANTASTIC BACK. His trigger reads "one or more tokens", so ELSPETH DOES NOT MULTIPLY HIM -- doubling a single creation event enters them simultaneously, which is one trigger, not two. Katara and Starfield DO, because doubling the trigger produces two separate token events. And he is a {3}{U} pip against 8 blue sources in a deck whose worst castability rows are already blue; he is also a non-Ally, so Katara doubles nothing of his own.
#~ -Sheriff of Safe Passage | +Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver | SIDEGRADE (owned M): {3} Equipment, +2/+2 and trample, and on combat damage to a player it makes a Treasure for EACH creature you control of a chosen type -- 24 Humans and 21 Allies here, so 5-6 off a normal board and 10-12 once Elspeth doubles them. Belladonna Took triggers PER token ("whenever A token enters"), so one connect completes her whole gain-then-draw-then-counters ladder. Sheriff is the cut because he is the one creature left that BOTH doublers are blind to: "enters with a +1/+1 counter for each other creature" is a replacement effect, not a triggered ability, and he is a Human Knight rather than an Ally.
#~ note: WHY ORCRIST IS PARKED RATHER THAN PLAYED, so the case is not re-derived: it is SIX mana before it does anything ({3} cast plus equip {3}) and it still has to connect, and the payoff is MANA this deck cannot spend -- avg MV 3.28, top end 5-6, and Great Divide Guide already turns every land AND every Ally into any-colour mana. United Front is the only {X} sink in the list. It is also the only Equipment, so there is no equip discount and nothing to tutor it. What WOULD change the answer: more {X} sinks or a real top end. The evasion objection is already answered -- Starry-Eyed Skyrider grants flying to an attacking creature and Enter the Avatar State grants flying plus hexproof for {W} -- so do not cut EITHER of those to make room for it.
