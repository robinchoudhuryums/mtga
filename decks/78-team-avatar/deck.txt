#: archetype: BANT ALLY TRIBAL, and the plan is a TRIGGER-DOUBLING engine rather than

#: archetype: this is a deck and not a theme — its ability draws a card the SECOND time
#: archetype: it resolves in a turn, so with Katara out every single Ally that enters
#: archetype: draws. The ETB Allies are chosen to be worth doubling: Earth Kingdom Jailer
#: archetype: exiles TWO permanents, The Earth King makes TWO 4/4 Bears, Ty Lee taps down
#: archetype: two blockers, Toph earthbends 4. Distinct from the roster's other Avatar
#: archetype: decks by axis, not just colour: 15 and 16 are WU tempo decks that happen to
#: archetype: be full of Allies (18 and 15 bodies against ONE and two payoffs), where
#: archetype: this deck is built on the payoff half and splashes green for it.
#: tier: B (PROVISIONAL) — an unplayed first draft, graded one band UNDER its A metrics
#: tier: floor on purpose. The floor reads A on interaction 7 and card advantage 4 at a
#: tier: 3.06 curve, and both numbers are honest as far as they go. What holds it at B is
#: tier: a single point of failure the metrics cannot price: the whole engine is one
#: tier: legendary 3/3. Katara, the Fearless is what turns a pile of small Allies into a
#: tier: draw engine, and against 2 protection effects one removal spell ends that. She is
#: tier: also the deck's worst castability row — {G}{W}{U} on turn three prices at ~51%
#: tier: against 13/8/8 sources, so realistically she lands turn four or five and the
#: tier: deck spends its first three turns deploying bodies that do not yet loop. Delney,
#: tier: Streetwise Lookout was ADDED 2026-08-31 as the partial answer — a {2}{W} second
#: tier: doubler covering the 14 of 22 trigger-holding creature copies at power 2 or less,
#: tier: which is every core engine piece — so the plan no longer needs Bant mana to work,
#: tier: only to work fully. Second
#: tier: cap: the interaction count is 8 but its composition is cheap tricks and auras
#: tier: (Into the Flood Maw, Path to Redemption, Crib Swap) rather than a removal
#: tier: suite, and `stats` flags zero answers to a noncreature permanent — that flag is
#: tier: itself a classifier under-read, since Earth Kingdom Jailer exiles an artifact or
#: tier: enchantment and Aang airbends any nonland permanent, so call the real figure two.
#: tier: Third: 46 distinct nonland cards is heavy singleton variance for a deck that
#: tier: wants a specific 3-drop. Re-grade after it is played — if Katara survives even
#: tier: half the time the A case is real, and that is a question no model here answers.
#: protect: Katara, the Fearless; South Pole Voyager; Avatar Enthusiasts; Haru, Hidden Talent; Suki, Kyoshi Warrior
#: notes: THE DOUBLER'S SCOPE is the thing to get right and it is narrower than "doubles
#: notes: Ally effects": Katara doubles TRIGGERED abilities only. White Lotus
#: notes: Reinforcements' anthem and Suki, Courageous Rescuer's +1/+0 are STATIC and are
#: notes: not doubled; Kyoshi Battle Fan's equip is an ACTIVATED ability and is not
#: notes: doubled (its ETB token is). Suki, Courageous Rescuer's token trigger reads
#: notes: "This ability triggers only once each turn", which caps it at one even with
#: notes: Katara out — she is in the list for the anthem and the body, not as an engine
#: notes: piece. Gather the White Lotus counts PLAINS, which is why the basics are 7
#: notes: Plains rather than an even split (G-75: a card that names a resource is worth
#: notes: the number of them in THIS deck).
#: notes: THE TWO DOUBLERS DO NOT COVER THE SAME CARDS. Katara reads ALLY and ignores
#: notes: power; Delney reads POWER 2 OR LESS and ignores type. They overlap on 14 of the
#: notes: 22 trigger-holding creature copies, and with both out a small Ally's trigger
#: notes: fires THREE times (base plus one each). The eight Delney misses are the bigger
#: notes: bodies: Earth Kingdom Jailer, Toph, both Appas, Suki Kyoshi Warrior, Kyoshi
#: notes: Warriors and Aang.
#: notes: LIVE TENSION, MEASURED, NOT YET RESOLVED (G-42 shape): this deck's own anthems
#: notes: push creatures OUT of Delney's range. White Lotus Reinforcements (+1/+1 to other
#: notes: Allies) and Suki, Courageous Rescuer (+1/+0 to other creatures) each take South
#: notes: Pole Voyager and Avatar Enthusiasts from 2 power to 3, and United Front puts a
#: notes: counter on every creature. TEN of the fourteen sit at EXACTLY power 2, so they
#: notes: are one anthem trigger from falling off; only Haru, Invasion Reinforcements,
#: notes: Jeong Jeong's Deserters and Forecasting Fortune Teller have headroom. Whether
#: notes: the anthems are a liability depends on how often a doubler is actually out,
#: notes: which is a play question — do not cut them on this note alone.
#: notes: Sonar Strike was CUT for Delney: its damage is conditional (attacking, blocking
#: notes: or tapped only) and its lifegain rider needs a Bat, of which this deck runs zero.
