#~ note: Katara, Water Tribe's Hope is PARKED, not rejected, and the number to re-check is the manabase. Measured 2026-09-01: {2}{W}{U}{U} against 9 blue sources (8 by identity; BS8-01 counts the any-colour land) is 43% on curve at turn five -- it would become the deck's worst castability row, below Katara the Fearless at 51%, which is the row the tier block already names as what holds this deck at B. Great Divide Guide (every land AND Ally taps for any colour) and Dazzling Theater (convoke pays a coloured pip off a blue body) both rescue her, but P(drawing either by turn five) is only 33.6%, before either resolves or survives. She comes in when blue sources go up -- roughly 18 are needed for a 90% double pip -- not when a slot opens.

#~ note: TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE BRINGING MISTER FANTASTIC BACK. His trigger reads "one or more tokens", so ELSPETH DOES NOT MULTIPLY HIM -- doubling a single creation event enters them simultaneously, which is one trigger, not two. Katara and Starfield DO, because doubling the trigger produces two separate token events. And he is a {3}{U} pip against 9 blue sources in a deck whose worst castability rows are already blue; he is also a non-Ally, so Katara doubles nothing of his own.
