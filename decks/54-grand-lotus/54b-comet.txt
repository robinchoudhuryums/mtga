#: archetype: FIREBENDING COMBAT-MANA (Temur plus a black splash for one card), and it is the THIRD reading of one clause.
#: archetype: Deck 54 uses Iroh's flashback as a DISCOUNT (a flat {1} on a fat Lesson).
#: archetype: Deck 54a uses it as a TRIGGER SOURCE (the payoff is the cast event). This list
#: archetype: uses it as a COMBAT REWARD, and its currency is mana that does not otherwise
#: archetype: exist. Same card, three decks — the 52/52a pattern taken one step further.
#: archetype: THE CLAUSE EVERYONE SKIMS IS THE LAST FOUR WORDS OF FIREBENDING: "whenever
#: archetype: this creature attacks, add {R}{R}. THIS MANA LASTS UNTIL END OF COMBAT." Mana
#: archetype: that expires at end of combat can only buy things castable at instant speed
#: archetype: DURING combat, which is normally a narrow and mostly-wasted resource. But
#: archetype: Iroh's grant reads "DURING YOUR TURN", and your combat phase is during your
#: archetype: turn. So the loop is: attack, generate four to ten red mana that would
#: archetype: otherwise evaporate, and spend it flashing back instants out of the graveyard
#: archetype: mid-combat. This deck is not cheating a cost and it is not being paid for a
#: archetype: cast — it is spending mana nobody else gets to spend.
#: archetype: THAT DICTATES THE COLOUR OF EVERY CARD, and it is the build rule to hold:
#: archetype: FIREBENDING MANA IS RED-ONLY AND COMBAT-ONLY, so the INSTANTS are red and the
#: archetype: blue and green pips sit on PERMANENTS you cast off lands in your main phase.
#: archetype: Blue is the graveyard half (Archmage's Newt, Slickshot Lockpicker, Sphinx of
#: archetype: Forgotten Lore) and green is two mana dorks and Iroh's pip. Nine red instants
#: archetype: are the combat-mana sinks.
#: archetype: TWO CARDS BREAK THE "UNTIL END OF COMBAT" LIMIT and they are the deck's real
#: archetype: engine. Electro, Assaulting Battery reads "you don't lose unspent red mana as
#: archetype: steps and phases end" — firebending mana stops expiring, so a big attack
#: archetype: bankrolls your SECOND main phase. The Last Agni Kai grants the same clause
#: archetype: until end of turn AND converts excess fight damage into more {R}. With either
#: archetype: out, combat mana becomes real mana.
#: archetype: FATED FIREPOWER IS THE CARD THIS DECK EXISTS TO CAST, and the reason is its
#: archetype: first word: FLASH. It was rejected twice for the other two lists because
#: archetype: {X}{R}{R}{R} is an {X} cost on triple red — but both objections dissolve here.
#: archetype: You cast it DURING combat off firebending mana, which is produced in fours and
#: archetype: fives and is red by definition, so X is large and the pips are free. Then
#: archetype: every damage source you control deals X more: firebending attackers, nine burn
#: archetype: instants, Thor, Thunderdrum Soloist (Guttersnipe was CUT for it — same
#: archetype: job, 3-damage upside when five-plus mana was spent, plus reach) and
#: archetype: Ovika's token swarm.
#: archetype: THE ATTACK IS THE DRAW STEP. Seven creatures turn combat into cards or spells:
#: archetype: Archmage's Newt grants flashback on damage and flashback {0} when SADDLED;
#: archetype: Sphinx of Forgotten Lore and The Dawning Archaic grant and free-cast on
#: archetype: attack; Seifer Almasy free-casts an MV 3-or-less spell on damage; Norman
#: archetype: Osborn is unblockable and connives; and Firebender Ascension counts every
#: archetype: attack trigger — firebending IS an attack trigger, so four firebenders swinging
#: archetype: fill it in ONE combat, after which it copies every trigger that follows.
#: archetype: DISTINCTIVENESS. vs 54: no fat Lessons, no Improvisation Capstone, an aggro
#: archetype: plan and a mana curve built around instants. vs 54a: 54a is a spell deck that
