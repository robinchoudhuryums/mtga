Held a band below the floor by three things the numbers can't see. 1. THE BLACK SPLASH IS 1 SOURCE for Beifong's Bounty Hunters / Dai Li Agents / Witherbloom — 16-18% on curve, and reaching even 65% would need 5 of 24 land slots for a color three cards use. The real fixing is creature-based (Vizier of the Menagerie spends any mana on creatures, and all three are creatures; Great Divide Guide taps every land and Ally for any color), so it works but both must survive. 2. SEVEN BASIC-LAND TUTORS AGAINST 7 BASICS — Earthbender Ascension, Bushwhack, Prismatic Undercurrents, The Earth King and Spider-Man all fetch a basic, and they run the deck dry. 3. Card advantage is 1, and the owned green/white pool has only two card-advantage cards at MV<=4, so that axis is not cheaply fixable in these colors. Singleton variance across the rainbow splash also caps it.
