#: name: Drawn Conclusions
#: format: Standard
#: tier: A — Dimir second-draw Villain midrange, now ON the A metrics floor rather than one band above it: interaction 8, card advantage 13, avg MV 3.31, protection 2. The 2026-10-08 tune bought the floor with answers, not volume — Glen Elendra Guardian, Confusticate and Bebother, Theorix Charm and Garruk, Veiled Butcher leave every answer but Garruk at instant speed, and six of the eight can hit a noncreature permanent. What the floor cannot see is the engine: nine "draw your second card each turn" payoffs turned on every turn by Super Intelligence's upkeep draw and Leader, Super-Genius's combat draw, plus the Villain-token loop (Madame Masque / Construct a Cosmic Cube make Villain tokens, Prowler connives off each). Weaknesses that keep it from S: protection 2, and black is the thin colour (13 sources) under the {B}{B} of Garruk, Uldaros Theorix and High-Society Hunter (about 75% on curve).
#: colors: UB
#: archetype: Dimir (U/B) "draw your second card each turn" Villain midrange. Cheap payoffs that grow or make tokens on the second draw (Kid Loki, Mischievous Mystic, Foggy Swamp Spirit Keeper, Kang, Madame Masque, Construct a Cosmic Cube), enablers that make the second draw automatic (Super Intelligence, Leader, the loot from Kaito, Cunning Infiltrator, Enduring Curiosity off evasive hits, Confusticate's instant-speed draw two on the OPPONENT's turn), cheap black removal and counters, and evasive bodies to close. DISTINCTNESS: the second-draw engine also runs in 79 (W/U, built on it) and 43 (Esper, its backup engine); this is the roster's only Dimir one, and the Villain-token angle is its own.
