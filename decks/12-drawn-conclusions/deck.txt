#: name: Drawn Conclusions
#: format: Standard
#: tier: A — Dimir second-draw Villain midrange with a self-discard layer, ON the A metrics floor: interaction 9, card advantage 12, avg MV 3.47, protection 2. Seven of the nine answers are instant speed (Garruk and Sephiroth's one-sided -2/-2 are the two that are not), and six can hit a noncreature permanent; Azula's enters-the-battlefield edict is a tenth the classifier does not read. Card advantage reads LOW against how the deck plays: Monument to Endurance's draw mode, Locke Cole's combat loot and Doctor Octopus's end-step refill to eight all sit outside or under that count. What the floor cannot see is the engine — six "draw your second card each turn" payoffs turned on every turn by Super Intelligence's upkeep draw and Leader, Super-Genius's combat draw; connive and loot discards (Leader, Kang, Prowler, Madame Masque, Emet-Selch, Locke Cole) that Monument to Endurance turns into a draw, a Treasure or three damage; and the Villain-token loop (Madame Masque / Construct a Cosmic Cube tokens make Prowler connive, and Doctor Octopus gives every Villain +2/+2). Weaknesses that keep it from S: protection 2, and black is the thin colour (13 sources) under the {B}{B} of Garruk, Uldaros Theorix and Azula (about 75% on curve) — the fix is a second untapped dual, not an Island-for-Swamp trade, which only moves the shortfall onto the {U}{U} cards.
#: colors: UB
#: archetype: Dimir (U/B) "draw your second card each turn" Villain midrange with a self-discard layer. Cheap payoffs that grow or make tokens on the second draw (Kid Loki, Mischievous Mystic, Foggy Swamp Spirit Keeper, Kang, Madame Masque, Construct a Cosmic Cube); enablers that make the second draw automatic (Super Intelligence, Leader, Emet-Selch, the loot from Kaito, Cunning Infiltrator, Enduring Curiosity off evasive hits, The Theorist's draw on the OPPONENT's draw step, Confusticate's instant-speed draw two); every connive and loot discard feeds Monument to Endurance, and a discarded fatty becomes a Superior Spider-Man target. Cheap black removal and counters hold the board; Doctor Octopus, Uldaros Theorix and Ultimecia's extra turn ({4}{U}{U}{B}{B} plus eight graveyard cards) are the late game. DISTINCTNESS: the second-draw engine also runs in 79 (W/U, built on it) and 43 (Esper, its backup engine); this is the roster's only Dimir one, and the Villain-token and discard angles are its own.
#: notes: Dimir (U/B) — second-draw Villain midrange. Tuned 2026-10-08 in two passes (22 swaps, see the flex block).
