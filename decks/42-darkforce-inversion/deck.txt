#: name: Darkforce Inversion
#: format: Standard
#: colors: WB
#: plan: control
#: archetype: Orzhov (W/B) BLINK-VALUE control. The plan is a life-total DIFFERENTIAL
#: archetype: turned into cards. Cheap value-ETB creatures are re-bought with flicker,
#: archetype: which also digs (Eerie Gravestone, Red Room Recruit, A.I.M. Synthoids) and
#: archetype: strips their hand (Deep-Cavern Bat, Virus Beetle, Corrupt Court Official);
#: archetype: lifelink bodies and a deep removal suite keep your total high while theirs
#: archetype: erodes. Mister Negative then exchanges totals — and because a blink is a
#: archetype: RE-ENTRY, flickering him TWICE draws you the whole gap and puts the life
#: archetype: totals back where they started (swap, draw the difference, swap back).
#: archetype: Another Round is the one card that does it alone: at X=1 it blinks your
#: archetype: board twice for {1}{1}{2}{W}.
#: archetype: DISTINCTNESS vs 20 Honor Among Thieves, the roster's other Orzhov deck:
#: archetype: 20 is a lifegain-drain AGGRO deck — evasive Ninjas on a clock, using life
#: archetype: as a Kingpin win-con. This is a CONTROL deck that treats life as a
#: archetype: RESOURCE to convert into cards, and wins by out-carding you. Deliberately
#: archetype: runs no Ninjas and no sneak/clock package; the creature base is chosen for
#: archetype: ETB text, not evasion.
#: tier: B — Orzhov blink-value control, one band BELOW the metrics floor of A, and
#: tier: deliberately so. The floor reads A on interaction 14 (12 of it instant-speed)
#: tier: over a 2.42 curve, and the interaction really is that deep — but the floor is
#: tier: blind to two things this deck genuinely lacks.
#: tier: 1. CARD ADVANTAGE reads 1, and while that UNDER-states the deck (Phyrexian
#: tier:    Arena, Enduring Innocence, Tolls of War's Clue, Eerie Gravestone's ETB draw
#: tier:    and Red Room Recruit's connive all draw ONE card at a
#: tier:    time, which is deliberately not counted), a pile of one-card draws is not
#: tier:    the same as a real engine. The deck out-values rather than out-draws until
#: tier:    Mister Negative lands.
#: tier: 2. RELIABILITY. The marquee payoff is a SINGLE copy of a 7-drop: ~22% to have
#: tier:    seen it by turn 7. The build is deliberately arranged so that is fine — every
#: tier:    blink target is a value ETB that stands on its own, so a Negative-less draw is
#: tier:    a normal Orzhov value deck rather than a dead hand — but a deck whose ceiling
#: tier:    shows up one game in five is not an A.
#: tier: Protection is 1 (Stolen Stark Tech) against 4 `#: protect:` build-arounds; the
#: tier: engine pieces are exposed, which is the standing risk of an enchantment/sorcery
#: tier: engine. PROVISIONAL — this is a fresh brew and has not been played; re-grade
#: tier: after some games. PATH TO A: a real card-advantage engine (something that draws
#: tier: 2+ at once), and a second copy of Another Round or Mister Negative to take the
#: tier: turn-7 hit rate from 22% to ~39%.
#: protect: Mister Negative; Another Round; Enduring Innocence; Salvation Swan
#: notes: WHY THE ENGINE WORKS — Mister Negative's ETB is a "may", and the draw clause
#: notes: fires only when YOUR life was higher. So: blink 1 swaps (you take their low
#: notes: total, draw the gap), blink 2 swaps back (you regain your high total, draw 0).
#: notes: Net across the pair: the whole differential in cards, life unchanged. From a
#: notes: LOW total the order inverts — blink 1 is a huge stabiliser, blink 2 buys the
#: notes: cards — so the engine works from either side; being high is just safer.
#: notes: The deck must FUNCTION WITHOUT HIM: every blink target is a value ETB that is
#: notes: fine on its own, so a Mister-Negative-less draw is a normal Orzhov value deck,
#: notes: not a dead hand. That is the difference between a build-around and a one-piece
#: notes: combo (he is a single copy of a 7-drop — ~22% to see him by turn 7).
#: notes: ROTATION: Deep-Cavern Bat (LCI) leaves Standard this fall. It is the single
#: notes: best blink target here, so it is in for now — replace it rather than rebuild.
#: notes: Craft targets: Mister Negative (M), Enduring Innocence (R), Case of the Uneaten
#: notes: Feast (R), Personify (U), Parting Gust (U), Getaway Glamer (U), Lifecreed Duo (C).
