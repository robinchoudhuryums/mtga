#: archetype: Dimir (U/B, black-primary) HEIST — the deck whose card advantage is
#: archetype: entirely the opponent's. It draws almost nothing of its own; it exiles

#: archetype: type pay for the stolen spell, so their colors are never a problem.
#: archetype: THE ENGINE IS COMBAT-TRIGGERED, which sets the whole shape. Tinybones the
#: archetype: Pickpocket, Black Widow, Reno and Rude, Shark Shredder and Etrata all
#: archetype: steal only when they CONNECT, so this is tempo-attrition midrange rather
#: archetype: than a durdling control deck: the creatures are cheap and hard to block

#: archetype: spells exist as much to CLEAR BLOCKERS as to answer threats. Nowhere to
#: archetype: Run does double duty — the -3/-3 kills a blocker and the static turns off
#: archetype: hexproof AND ward, so a warded blocker can't wall the engine.
#: archetype: Tinybones, Bauble Burglar is the second engine, and note the precise
#: archetype: wording: it steals what an opponent DISCARDS, not what they exile. So the
#: archetype: disruption package is built from real discard — Virus Beetle, Corrupt
#: archetype: Court Official, Ninja of the Hand's power-up, Psychic Whorl, Ozai's
#: archetype: Cruelty, Auntie's Sentence and both Jecht chapters — seven sources that
#: archetype: turn into thefts with Burglar out and are fine disruption without him.
#: archetype: Exile-from-hand effects were deliberately NOT included for this reason:
#: archetype: they put the card beyond everyone's reach, which is off-thesis here.
#: archetype: NO GRAVEYARD HATE, for the mirror-image reason. Strategic Betrayal and Pit of
#: archetype: Offerings were both CUT after the full-text read — they read as fine cards
#: archetype: alone, but they EXILE an opponent's graveyard, and four heist cards here
#: archetype: (Tinybones the Pickpocket, Shark Shredder, Hama and Azula) need it FULL.
#: archetype: Hecteyes took the slot, which is the inversion: discard FILLS the graveyard
#: archetype: those four steal from.
#: archetype: BLUE IS A DELIBERATE TWO-CARD SPLASH — only Azula and Etrata need a real

#: archetype: black alone, so a blue source is never a dead draw even when neither blue

#: archetype: hand and graveyard. The `heist` theme (82 pool cards, 0.52%) is carried by
#: archetype: 14 cards here and by no other deck on the roster.
#: tier: B — Dimir heist tempo-attrition, one band BELOW the A metrics floor, and
#: tier: PROVISIONAL (unplayed brew, 18 craft targets). The floor reads A on interaction
