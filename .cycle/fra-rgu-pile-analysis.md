## 1. The decision framework

Context measured before grading: no red-green or Temur deck in the roster plays Heartwood
or artifact tokens. Nearest neighbours: 49 Big Draco (mono-red Dragons), 59 Stampede
(red-green, attacking makes mana), 30/35/54 (Temur, other plans).

- **R1 — The currency is ARTIFACT TOKENS.** Reality Fracture's red-green mechanic is the
  **Heartwood** token (a red/green mana artifact). Grade a maker by how REPEATABLE it is:
  - Woodwork Prodigy re-prepares itself every upkeep, so it makes one every turn.
  - Eusocial Engineering makes a Robot per land drop.
  - Chandra, Spark Hunter makes a Vehicle with her 0.
  - Rocketeer Boostbuggy makes a Treasure per attack.

  One-shot makers (Konstrari Improviser, Heartwood Crafter, Puppetbeast, Biotech's Lander,
  Sandstorm Salvager's Golem) are filler that also fixes mana.
- **R2 — Draconic Visitor turns every artifact-token creation into a 5/5 flying Dragon.**
  That makes the deck a threat engine instead of a ramp deck, but it is ONE card. Every maker
  must stand on its own without it, and its value is read as "how many makers does the deck
  hold" (G-61), not from its own text.
- **R3 — Visitor FIGHTS the artifact-COUNT payoffs (G-42).** Its Dragons are creature
  tokens, NOT artifacts, and they replace the artifact. So with Visitor out:
  - Craterclaw Colossus's +X, Aerid's {6} +X, Tenured Tethermage's "tap two artifacts" and
    the sac-an-artifact outlets (Puppetbeast, Biotech, Spark Hunter +2) all stop being fed.
  - The Heartwood mana is lost too.

  Two different decks hide in this pile: a "go wide on artifacts" deck and a "forge Dragons"
  deck. They share makers but not payoffs.
- **R4 — Wizards is a G-59 trap.** Plenty of Wizard BODIES (Gandalf, Viashino Pyromancer,
  Stingcaster, Unsparing Boltcaster, Goliath Daydreamer, Radagast, Terra, Semester
  Foreseer, Lyra, Ruric Thar, Fatehold Chronologist, plus Cadet tokens). Wizard PAYOFFS:
  Command the Stage (+1/+1 on other Wizard TOKENS only) and Gandalf's Flameshape (play the
  exiled cards if you control any Wizard): **two**. Wizards is incidental, not an axis.
- **R5 — Heartwoods are ramp, so the top end is affordable.** Pyrewood Gearhulk (6),
  Craterclaw Colossus (7), Craftwork Crusher (7), Savage Ventmaw (6) and Emrakul (10) are
  real targets for a deck making a mana artifact most turns. Count makers before trusting it.
- **R6 — A prepared copy is a SPELL cast.** Soul Tether, Peer Review and Molten Tide copies
  count as noncreature spells for Saheeli (Thopter), Gandalf (ping), Winter, Team Player
  (+1/+0) and Geist's discount. Hexhaven Dueling Arena re-prepares a creature.
- **R7 — A third colour must clear a measured bar** (the Bant pile failed on mana). The
  pile's own blue duals are the Commons (Innovative UR, Transformative GU), untapped only
  with a planeswalker; the red-green ones are Rockfall Vale (fast-ish) and Konstrari Annex
  (planeswalker).
- **R8 — Superfriends overlaps deck 81.** Chandra Torch, Spark Hunter, Ajani Unrelenting,
  Pyromancer, Warlord and Command the Stage are already deck 81's core. A walker package
  here is fine, but an RG superfriends deck would be deck 81 recoloured.

## 3. Cross-batch observations

- **The real axis is Heartwood artifacts, with Draconic Visitor as the payoff that changes
  the deck's shape (R1/R2).**
- **Dragons are a real sub-theme once Visitor exists:**
  - natural Dragons: Visitor, Bloomvine Regent, Sagu Wildling, Savage Ventmaw, Traxos
    (Dragon Construct), Kiora's tokens;
  - payoffs: Bloomvine (3 life per Dragon entering), Encroaching Dragonstorm (returns to hand
    on a Dragon, re-buying its ramp), Molten/Piercing Exhale (behold a Dragon).
- **Blue's best addition is Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar:** every noncreature spell makes a
  Thopter, and with Visitor each Thopter becomes a 5/5 Dragon. The rest of blue is either the
  Jace / superfriends shell (deck 81's lane, R8) or spell-prowess bodies.
