## 1. The decision framework

- **F1 — Three payoff populations decide everything (G-59).** Count separately:
  (a) DWARF payoffs (cards that name Dwarves), (b) EQUIPMENT payoffs ("equipped
  creature you control", "whenever you attach", equip-cost reducers), (c) TREASURE
  payoffs (produce or spend). A card is graded against the population it feeds.
- **F2 — An Equipment's REAL cost is cast + equip.** Free-attach effects (ETB-attach,
  job select, "attach without paying") change the math; count the attach-cheats before
  grading any expensive equip cost. Equipment target density in a 60 is ~8-12; the pile
  holds ~50 equipment, so most are cut on RATE vs best-in-slot, not on theme.
- **F3 — Colors W/R (G-58):** grade printed COST; hybrids on-color; identity flags are
  not castability verdicts (standing error E1 in the Wylie doc — do not repeat it).
- **F4 — Read the bridge cards' actual text before assuming the bridge.** Magda is
  reputed the Dwarf→Treasure bridge; verify her OTJ text in the pull, not from memory.
- **F5 — Vigilance/untap matters for equip costs**: equip is sorcery-speed; a deck that
  attacks with equipment wants vigilant bodies or cheap re-equips.
- **F6 — Variant signals to track:** (a) TREASURE value (user-flagged), (b) the
  Samurai/Katana cluster, (c) "attacks alone"/solo-attack cluster, (d) anything that
  overlaps deck 38 Armory's identity (equipment voltron) — same-identity risk, not a
  variant.

## 3b. Cross-batch observations (final)

- **The Dwarf engine is trigger-based, not voltron** — Kíli (free first equip + draw per
  Dwarf/Equipment ETB), Giott (loot per Dwarf AND Equipment ETB), Balin (wheel-draw +
  story burn), Fíli (2/2 per nontoken Dwarf + anthem), with **Bifur doubling every Dwarf
  trigger** and **Cloud doubling every Equipment trigger** on the equipped creature.
  This is what keeps it DISTINCT from deck 38 Armory (voltron): the equipment here are
  cheap trigger fodder, not a suit for one creature.
- **STORIED is nearly free here** (3+ artifacts/legendaries/Sagas): Fíli/Thorin O./
  Bifur/Kíli/Ori/Óin/Dáin L. all switch on by turn 3-4.
- **Mjölnir's "equip worthy {1}"** — worthy = legendary non-Villain red/white — covers
  essentially every Dwarf legend: a {1}-equip damage doubler.
- **The Dwarf→Treasure bridges:** Orcrist (combat damage → Treasure per creature of a
  chosen type — name Dwarf), Dori (ETB Treasure), Magda (crime → Treasure; sac 3 →
  a 4/4 Dragon), Diamond Pick-Axe / Goldvein Pick / Gold Pan on the equipment side.
- **VARIANT — TREASURE HOARD (user-flagged, viable):** Smaug (upkeep Treasure; attack →
  damage = Treasure count), The Misty Mountains Cold (4 Treasures → 6/6 Dragon),
  Magda's Dragon, plus the producer suite (Rapacious Dragon, Brass's Bounty, Hell to
  Pay, Seize the Spoils, Season of the Bold, Stark Industries Executive, Prompto,
  Death to Our Enemies, J. Jonah Jameson, Involuntary Employment, Luxurious
  Locomotive, The Gold Saucer). Dwarves dig treasure, dragons come — the Hobbit
  flavor writes itself. Payoff count clears G-59.
- **NOT a variant:** the Samurai/Katana cluster (Leonardo, Colleen Wing, the Katanas)
  has ~1 real payoff; the tap-down equipment (Web-Shooters, Thunder Lasso, Captain
  America's Shield, Skateboard, Trusty Boomerang) belong to the Wylie-family decks.
